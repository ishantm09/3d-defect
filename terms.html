What the service is

Detechrm is a free student project that uses a machine-learning model to flag possible 3D-printing defects from images.

The model can miss defects or flag problems that are not there. Treat results as a helpful hint, not a guarantee. Always check your print yourself, and never leave a 3D printer running unattended on the basis of this tool alone.
