Detechrm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Detechrm is a student project that helps spot 3D-printing defects (spaghetti, stringing and warping) from a photo or a live camera feed. This page explains what data the site handles.

Photos and camera

The detection model runs inside your own browser. Photos you upload and frames from your camera are analysed on your device and are not sent to our servers. We do not receive, store or view them.

Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep each account's history separate. We do not receive your Google password.

What is stored on your device

  • Your scan and live-monitor history, including the last few small captured frames.
  • Your settings, such as theme and alert preferences.
  • Your sign-in details (name and email), if you signed in.

All of this stays in your browser's local storage. You can remove it with the Clear button in History or by clearing your browser data.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not run advertising.
  • We do not keep a database of users or photos.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. Google sign-in is provided by Google and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Detechrm is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
